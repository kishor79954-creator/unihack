# NEXUS PI: Demo Script

## 🎯 Target Audience
Hackathon Judges (Technical & Business focus)

## ⏱️ Target Duration
5 Minutes

---

## 0:00 - The Problem
**[Screen]** Dashboard (Executive view showing empty/fragmented catalog metrics if possible, or start on the high-level dashboard).
**[Speaker]** "Industrial manufacturers sit on thousands of fragmented product data points across PDFs, spreadsheets, and supplier sites. The problem isn't just extracting it—it's trusting it, validating it, connecting it, and getting it ready for commerce."

## 0:30 - The Solution & Catalog Import
**[Screen]** Dashboard → Click 'Import Catalog' (or show existing Hero Product)
**[Speaker]** "NEXUS PI is an intelligence layer that sits between fragmented data and commerce systems. We don't just use RAG to search; we use Document Intelligence, Knowledge Graphs, and Human Review to build verified, commerce-ready catalogs."

## 1:00 - Product Intelligence & Evidence (The "Aha!" Moment)
**[Screen]** Navigate to Hero Product (SKF 6205-2RS). Tab: Specifications.
**[Speaker]** "Let's look at this industrial bearing. The AI has extracted technical specifications. But notice the 'Trust Status'. It hasn't just guessed."
**[Action]** Click on the 'Evidence' link next to Bore Diameter.
**[Speaker]** "NEXUS PI links every attribute directly to the source PDF, page, and exact bounding box where it found the fact. If it's low confidence, it gets flagged for human review."

## 2:10 - The Knowledge Graph
**[Screen]** Navigate to Knowledge Graph tab.
**[Speaker]** "Industrial products are relational. By plotting this into a Neo4j Knowledge Graph, the system inherently understands that this SKF bearing is an exact alternative to an NTN bearing, and what machines it is compatible with."

## 2:40 - Ask NEXUS AI (Grounded Investigation)
**[Screen]** Open AI Copilot drawer on the product page.
**[Action]** Ask: "What are the verified specifications for this bearing?"
**[Speaker]** "Because our AI is grounded in the Knowledge Graph and validated attributes, it doesn't hallucinate. It tells us exactly what is verified."
**[Action]** Ask: "What information is missing?"
**[Speaker]** "It can instantly perform a gap analysis against required taxonomy standards."

## 3:30 - Autonomous Enrichment
**[Screen]** Click 'Improve Product' button.
**[Action]** Show the Before/After Enrichment UI.
**[Speaker]** "Based on the missing data, the AI proposes an enrichment plan. It drafts technical and SEO descriptions based *only* on the verified facts."

## 4:40 - Fact-Check & Publishing
**[Screen]** Click 'Approve & Publish'. Show the preflight check.
**[Speaker]** "Before anything hits a live storefront, NEXUS PI runs a final fact-check and validation preflight. Only products with 100% resolved critical conflicts can be published."
**[Action]** Show 'Published' success state.

## 5:00 - Conclusion
**[Speaker]** "NEXUS PI transforms raw PDFs into trusted, interconnected, commerce-ready intelligence. Thank you."
