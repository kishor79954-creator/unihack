# NEXUS PI: Presentation Pitch

## 1. The Hook (Problem)
"Every year, industrial distributors and manufacturers waste millions of hours copying specifications from PDFs into databases just to sell products online. And yet, this data remains fragmented, unverified, and disconnected."

## 2. The Solution (NEXUS PI)
"Meet NEXUS PI: an AI-powered intelligence layer. We don't just extract text. We understand it, we validate it with hard evidence, we map its relationships into a knowledge graph, and we prepare it for commerce."

## 4. Innovation & Differentiation
"Why are we different? 
1. **Evidence-Backed AI**: Every fact is traced to a bounding box in a source document. 
2. **Knowledge Graph**: We don't just do semantic search; we map exact compatibility.
3. **Deterministic Publishing**: AI can propose content, but it cannot publish unverified conflicts."

## 5. Business Impact
"For an enterprise, NEXUS PI means getting products online weeks faster. It means buyers can trust the specs, resulting in fewer returns. It's the difference between a chatbot that guesses, and a platform that governs."
